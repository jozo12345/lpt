      Home screen

      Tick the mosques whose jamā‘ah times show on this phone's home screen.

      Reminders

      Jamā‘ah reminders are for the mosques ticked under Home screen. Each type has its own sound; Last call sounds like a warning.

      If reminders arrive late, stop the phone's battery saver from holding the app back: Settings → Apps → Salah Times → Battery → Unrestricted. On Samsung, also remove it from Sleeping apps (Settings → Battery → Background usage limits).

      Timetables on file

      Start times arrive by themselves every Monday, up to a year ahead. Jamā‘ah times are added month by month when each mosque publishes them.

      Ramadan
      
        During these dates the app turns rose-coloured, counts the days of Ramadan, calls each mosque's ‘Ishā jamā‘ah Taraweeh, and the Month page shows Ramadan 1–30. The date is filled in from the calendar; change it once the moon is sighted.
